# For a festival's safety team

Fieldwatch gives the people on your grounds the weather that is coming, and your words beside it. This is what you get,
what you do, and what it costs you: nothing, and no accounts.

## What your people get

- Warnings and watches from the National Weather Service for your grounds, not the county line, on a phone with the app
  closed, within seconds of issue.
- A lightning code on the festival safety protocol (red under 8 miles, orange to 12, yellow to 20) from the GOES lightning
  mapper, graded every 20 seconds, with the nearest flash and the all-clear countdown. Your own detection and your staff stay
  the authority; the app says so on the screen.
- A heads-up hours before the forecast turns stormy, windy, wet or dangerously hot, with the first thing to do and a countdown.
- A radar loop that plays with no signal, a forecast built for a field (heat index, gusts, thunder), and what the rain will do
  to your ground (mud, standing water, slick blacktop).
- Your updates and evacuation notices as alerts, pushed like a warning.

## What you do

1. **Get your staff key** from the Fieldwatch admin, with a link that opens the app on your festival in staff mode. The key
   is shown once. Put it in Settings on each staff phone (five taps on the credits line opens the staff settings).
2. **Post updates** from Settings, Post an update: a title, a line or two, and how loud (heads up, warning). It reaches every
   phone that favorited the festival, and the festival page, at once.
3. **Review hazard reports** people send from the app before they go out, from Settings, Review reports.
4. **Correct the grounds** on the Ground screen: surface, soil, low ground, camping, what is standing. Pin it here moves the
   pin to where you stand. The lookups get most of this right; you fix the rest once.
5. **Print the QR code** (Share on the festival page) at the gates and on the screens.

## What it does not do

- No accounts, no logins, no names. The key is the festival's, not a person's. Phones register nothing but a push token
  and the festival they follow.
- Nothing replaces carrier emergency alerts or your own lightning detection; the app tells people to keep both on.
- No operations radio. The receiver node, where one is used, records county public-safety radio only.
